# Supply Chain

Modern OSS adoption depends heavily on understanding what is included in
the software and where it comes from. SBOMs, license traceability, and
dependency management are increasingly important for regulatory
compliance, customer assurance, and security risk reduction.

This area is particularly relevant for CRA-related expectations and
downstream product integration.

## Scope

Supply Chain includes:

- dependency identification
- dependency minimization
- dependency monitoring
- third-party component tracking
- SBOM generation
- license traceability
- provenance information
- component update processes
- supply-chain risk assessment
- evidence needed by downstream users and auditors
