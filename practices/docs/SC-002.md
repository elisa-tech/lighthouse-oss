# Maintain SBOMs and licensing traceability

## Evidence

Practice "Maintain SBOMs and licensing traceability" from *2025 - Towards
a more sustainable and secure software tooling in free/libre open source
software environments (S. Tatschner)*, 2.2.4. Legal Regulations / 3.2.
Tracking Down Software Cluster Bombs.

> [Page 25] “As the number of dependencies listed in SBOM documents can
> be very high, managing these dependencies will become a crucial part
> of establishing effective measures in a cybersecurity governance
> process.”

> [Page 67] "Especially due to new legal requirements such as the
> European Cyber Resilience Act, there is the need for software projects
> to keep a transparent track record with SBOM and maintain a good
> overall state."

## Open Discussion

Comment in the WIPPractices sheet:

> I have doubts about keeping maintenance of SBOMs as part of this
> point. If a downstream vendor uses an OSS in a commercial product,
> that vendor must include SBOMs and vulnerability documentation in its
> technical dossier. An OSS project might cooperate, provide
> vulnerability metadata or guidance, but they don't have an obligation
> or duty to maintain SBOMs. So, I am now less inclined to keep it as is
> as a best/good practice. It may still be good practice for an OSS
> project to publish a "release manifest" or SBOM-like output (component
> list, known CVEs, etc.) proactively. What do you think?
>
> -Wendi Urribarri
