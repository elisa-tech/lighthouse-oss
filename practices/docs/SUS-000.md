# Sustainability

Sustainability describes whether the project can continue to operate,
maintain itself, respond to issues, and support users over time. A project
may be technically excellent but still risky for downstream adoption if it
depends on too few people, lacks funding, or cannot sustain maintenance.

Sustainability is especially important for critical OSS components used in
industrial, safety-relevant, or long-lived products.

## Scope

Sustainability includes:

- funding model
- maintainer capacity
- bus factor
- contributor retention
- organizational support
- infrastructure continuity
- release continuity
- long-term maintenance planning
- succession planning
- resilience against loss of key contributors or sponsors
