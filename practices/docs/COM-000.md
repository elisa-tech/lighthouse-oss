# Community Health

A project can have good formal governance but still be unhealthy if there
are too few active contributors, weak support channels, poor
responsiveness, or excessive dependency on a small group of maintainers.

Community Health is therefore a key signal for long-term usability and
trust.

## Scope

Community Health includes:

- active contributor base
- contributor diversity and participation
- public communication channels
- support channels
- user and contributor responsiveness
- community onboarding
- organizational participation
- mentoring and knowledge sharing
- visible community activity
