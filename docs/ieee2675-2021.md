# IEEE Standard for DevOps

Building Reliable and Secure Systems Including Application Build, Package, and Deployment

## Direct inclusion of Open Source
The standard mainly refers to OSS for dependency handling as could be understood for SBOM

## Mentioned OSS principles

- collaboration and communication in groups
- considers full life cycle
- handles sub systems (components of larger stacks)
- maintainers are mentioned

## Where it may help
- defines processes and methods
- describes a lot of requirements put on the life cycle of software

## Other thoughts
- mainly considered for DevOPs in companies, but also applicable to individual projects
- traceability, left-shift,

and Evaluation (SQuaRE) - Part 3: Quality in use.
- ISO/IEC/IEEE 12207:2017 for Systems and software engineering — Software life cycle processes
